Project / 01 — AI Campaign

Night Drive

ClientDiesel
Year2024
RoleCreative Direction, AI Image Generation, Retouching

Concept

A campaign built around motion and artificial light — a tunnel sequence generated frame by frame until the color temperature, reflections, and blur read as a single continuous drive rather than a static render. The brief called for a Diesel-adjacent night-time energy: fast, a little dangerous, unmistakably urban.

Detail — placeholder Study — placeholder

Process / Notes

Early passes leaned too clean — the tunnel lighting looked rendered rather than driven through. Reintroducing motion blur and asymmetric exposure across the frame sold the speed.

Color grading went through six iterations before landing on the amber/teal split that reads as “night drive” without tipping into generic cyberpunk.
