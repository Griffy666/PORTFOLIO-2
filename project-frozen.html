Project / 12 — Concept / Art Direction

Frozen

ClientSelf-Initiated
Year2024
RoleCreative Direction, AI Image Generation, Concept Development

Concept

A bag suspended inside a block of ice — a concept that only exists because AI removed the production barrier between the idea and the image. The brief: make it look like it was actually shot that way, not composited after the fact.

Detail — placeholder Study — placeholder

Process / Notes

The first generations read as an obvious overlay — ice needed internal fracture and refraction, not just a frosted filter, to sell the illusion.

Lighting was rebuilt from a single hard key to mimic how a real studio would light a frozen object, which is what finally made the concept believable.
